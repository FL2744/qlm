A small space for words

A page can begin with a sentence, a picture, or a question. qlm keeps the document close at hand: open a file, write, and save.

English and Arabic can share the same document.

A note for the web

The document is the starting point. Add an image to make HTML, or open an existing page and edit its rendered text. Switch to source whenever the markup matters.

Rewriting and translation are deliberate actions. Select a passage, ask for a change, and compare the result with the original.
